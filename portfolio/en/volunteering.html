PUCRS Carreiras · Since 2025 · 20+ mentees

UX career mentoring

As a volunteer mentor at PUCRS Carreiras, I’ve mentored 20+ PUCRS students who want to break into or grow in UX. Each of them was at a different stage: some still figuring out what UX is, some switching careers, some already designing and going after their first role.

Every mentoring session starts like a project: by listening. Understanding where someone is decides whether we talk about the market, their portfolio or their next step.

Why I do it

Explaining why someone’s case study isn’t working forces me to look at mine with the same eyes. I’m a better designer for it.
